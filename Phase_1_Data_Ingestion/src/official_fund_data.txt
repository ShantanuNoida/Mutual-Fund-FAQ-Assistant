## AMFI Knowledge Center: SIP
- Definition: A method of investing a fixed sum in a mutual fund scheme at regular intervals (monthly/quarterly).
- Key Benefits: Rupee Cost Averaging, Power of Compounding, Disciplined Investing.
- Minimum Investment: Many schemes allow starting an SIP with as little as ₹500 per month.
- Source: https://www.amfiindia.com/investor/knowledge-center-info?zoneName=sip

## AMFI Knowledge Center: Taxation (FY 2024-25)
- Equity-Oriented Funds STCG: 20% if units held for 12 months or less.
- Equity-Oriented Funds LTCG: 12.5% on gains exceeding ₹1.25 lakh if units held for more than 12 months.
- Non-Equity Oriented Funds (Debt/Gold): Taxed at investor's applicable income tax slab rates.
- Dividend Tax: Added to investor's income and taxed at slab rates. TDS at 10% if dividend exceeds ₹5,000.
- Source: https://www.amfiindia.com/investor/knowledge-center-info?zoneName=TaxationMutualFunds
